01How it works

Radar brings people in.
SquadUp makes each one bring more.

The first hundred sign-ups rarely come from posting louder. They come from the group admins who already have your audience.

i

Radar ranks the groups

It scores every club and community in the directory on shared topics, city and size, and explains each match in a line.

ii

A person approves every message

Drafts are written in English, Hindi or Marathi from your event details only. Nothing goes to an admin until someone on our team has read and approved it.

iii

Students join in chat

A tap on the tracked link opens the bot. Language, role, experience: three taps and they're in, with the group that sent them on record.

iv

Squads fill themselves

Everyone is seated where their skill is missing. Friends they invite get first claim on open seats, and reminders keep no-shows down.

02Safety

Reach that doesn't feel like spam.

  • Nothing sends itself

    Every message is read and approved by a person before it reaches a group admin.

  • Admins, not inboxes

    Messages go to group admins, who decide whether to forward. No cold DMs, no mass messaging.

  • Only facts you gave us

    Messages use your event's name, date, venue and description. No invented numbers or urgency.

  • First names on shared cards

    Team cards travel through group chats, so they never carry more than a first name and a role.
